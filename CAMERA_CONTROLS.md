# Camera Controls

The software rasterizer includes a controllable camera system with two modes:

## FPS Mode (First-Person Shooter)
- **W** - Move forward (where camera is looking)
- **S** - Move backward
- **A** - Strafe left
- **D** - Strafe right
- **Left Click** - Capture mouse for camera control
- **Mouse Movement** - Look around (when mouse is captured)
- **ESC** - Release mouse capture

## Orbit Mode
- **WASD** - Zoom in/out
- **Left Click** - Capture mouse for orbit control
- **Mouse Movement** - Orbit around target (when mouse is captured)
- **Mouse Wheel** - Zoom in/out
- **ESC** - Release mouse capture

## General Controls
- **C** - Switch between FPS and Orbit camera modes
- **R** - Toggle cube rotation

## Tips
- Click anywhere in the window to capture the mouse for camera control
- Press ESC to show the mouse cursor again
- In FPS mode, mouse controls camera rotation
- In Orbit mode, the camera orbits around the origin (0,0,0)
